Rates

  • Half Day, Starting at $950.

    Up to 4 hours on location and about 30 edited photos, delivered in a private online gallery.

  • Full Day, Starting at $1,750.

    A full day on location for bigger properties, multiple vehicles or a longer trip.

  • AI Ad Variations, $350.

    An optional add-on: a set of ad variations made with AI tools from your shoot’s photos. Each one is labeled as AI-made, so you always know which is which.

Travel is extra: $0.76 a mile by car, flights and hotels quoted up front. A 50% deposit books your date, and the balance is due on delivery. You can use the photos in your own marketing, including your website, social, print and email, for as long as you like. Paid ads, billboards, packaging and resale are licensed separately.   3. I shoot on location. You get a private proof gallery, pick your favorites, and I deliver the finished photos.
